About PixoraSeal

PixoraSeal was founded by Saurabh Yadav with a simple idea — product photos need proof, not just pixels.

Shopify sellers spend time and money on original product photography, but when AI-generated images flood the market, buyers and platforms cannot tell what is real. PixoraSeal gives every photo a server-signed proof — SHA-256 hash, signature, certificate and a verify link — so original work can be proven anytime.

Our Mission: Make product photo authenticity simple, verifiable and affordable for small sellers, not just big brands.

How it works: Upload a photo → Our server creates its hash and signs it → You get a certificate and verify link. Anyone can re-upload the photo to check if it is original.

We are building for global sellers, with honesty first — we show what our proof is, and what it is not. Adobe Content Credentials JUMBF support is planned for Phase 2.

— Saurabh Yadav, Founder, PixoraSeal
