Last updated: 9 Nov 2026

What PixoraSeal Does

PixoraSeal provides server-side proof for product photos. When you seal a photo, our server calculates its SHA-256 hash, creates a claim ID, timestamps it, and signs the hash with our private key. You get a manifest and certificate as proof.

Important: PixoraSeal does not currently embed Adobe Content Credentials JUMBF data inside the image file, and our seals are not verified by verify.contentauthenticity.org. Our proof is our own server-signed manifest. Do not claim Adobe C2PA verification based on PixoraSeal alone.
